GLORIOUS HISTORY
OF THE MANDAL

Sahyadri Krida Mandal • Est. 1976
✦  •  ✦

Sahyadri Krida Mandal was established in 1976 at Tilak Nagar Samaj Mandir Auditorium, opposite Building 22, Lokmanya Tilak Kridangan, Tilak Nagar, Chembur, Mumbai - 400089. Since its inception, local residents, passionate youth, and senior guides have come together to preserve and enrich the revered public Ganeshotsav tradition.

✦  •  ✦

Over the decades, the sacred idol form of 'Sahyadri Krida Mandal' became the cherished darling of millions of devotees across the globe. The idol's serene royal posture, the grand hand-carved golden throne, and the awe-inspiring thematic mandap creations are universally celebrated as the hallmarks of our beloved Raja.

॥ GANPATI BAPPA MORYA ॥
